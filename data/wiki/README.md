# Guild Wars Wiki revision evidence

These are read-only snapshots used to verify MappingOut's game content. Each page records its title, page and revision IDs, revision timestamp, source URL, permanent revision URL, retrieval timestamp, and original wikitext. Original contributors are credited through the page history at `https://wiki.guildwars.com/index.php?title=<URL-encoded title>&action=history`; the application links both the fixed revision and its history.

Wiki original contributions are available under GNU Free Documentation License 1.3 or later. The license text is bundled at `public/licenses/gfdl-1.3.txt`. ArenaNet game content retains its original rights and is not relicensed by inclusion in a Wiki snapshot. See the saved `Guild Wars Wiki:Copyrights` page and <https://wiki.guildwars.com/wiki/Guild_Wars_Wiki:Copyrights>.

Candidate matching, image comparisons, and downloaded pages are research evidence. Only entries included in the compiled corrections are applied to the app; a snapshot alone does not establish that a match or coordinate is confirmed. See `docs/wiki-data-review.md` and the generated public review report for current coverage and remaining work.
